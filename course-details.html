About This Course

This course introduces you to the essential concepts and tools needed to build websites. You will develop your understanding step by step and explore how different technologies work together.

What You'll Learn

By the end of this course, you will understand the following topics:

  • Understand fundamental web technologies.
  • Create responsive web pages.
  • Apply your knowledge through practical exercises.

Requirements

  • No previous experience required.
  • A computer and internet connection.
  • Willingness to learn and practice.
